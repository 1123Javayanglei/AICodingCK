练习一（原书第 9–20 题，仅保留已作答题目）

13. ____ you want some samples of our new products, you might contact our sales manager.
A. If    B. While    C. Until    D. Although
答案：A

14. Not until I got to the airport ____ that my passport was missing.
A. had I realized    B. will I realize    C. I will realize    D. I had realized
答案：A

15. As a sales person, ____ John lacks is self-confidence when he is talking with clients.
A. which    B. what    C. why    D. that
答案：B

16. If I ____ his email address, I would have sent him a message yesterday.
A. have known    B. had known    C. will know    D. know
答案：B

17. The more satisfied your employees are, the more they will contribute ____ the success of your company.
A. on    B. about    C. with    D. to
答案：D

18. He read my credit card number out loud in front of customers ____ I could stop him.
A. before    B. while    C. as    D. since
答案：A

练习二（原书第 1–20 题，仅保留已作答题目）

1. Without a sufficient amount of reliable data and evidence, no justifiable ____ can be drawn. This is the scientific spirit.
A. reasons    B. approvals    C. sources    D. conclusions
答案：D

2. A UN spokesman said yesterday that extra troops would be sent to that region to ____ UN peacekeepers there.
A. represent    B. resolve    C. regain    D. reinforce
答案：D

3. Without further proof, the police had to ____ the man under arrest, who happily went home.
A. reveal    B. receive    C. release    D. relax
答案：C

4. Our English teacher is a man with keen sense of responsibility. He ____ the performance of each student in the oral practice carefully.
A. evaluated    B. estimated    C. judged    D. assumed
答案：A

5. It was inadequate preparations that ____ his failure in the exam, but he said that the exam was poorly designed.
A. lead to    B. expose to    C. attribute to    D. result to
答案：A

9. The Johnsons' cat likes to ____ the mice as if it were playing with them.
A. run    B. travel    C. pursue    D. chase
答案：D

10. I'm very busy. I can't ____ three days away from work. You'd better invite another person to go with you.
A. spend    B. stand    C. offer    D. afford
答案：D

11. ____ is often the case with a new idea, much preliminary activity and optimistic discussion produce no concrete proposals.
A. That    B. It    C. Which    D. As
答案：D

12. ____ nobody has anything more to say, let's round off the discussion.
A. When    B. While    C. So that    D. Now that
答案：D

13. I was persuaded to do the job, ____ I didn't want to.
A. lest    B. in order that    C. even though    D. in case
答案：C

14. He ran ____ no one could catch him.
A. so fast    B. very fast that    C. that fast so    D. so fast that
答案：D

15. ____ he will not accept the offer, I have to find somebody else.
A. Until    B. Though    C. As soon as    D. Seeing that
答案：D

16. You must practice daily ____ you will win the race.
A. so that    B. unless    C. when    D. while
答案：A

17. Let's talk all this over again ____ we make a final decision.
A. after    B. before    C. while    D. as
答案：B

18. All matter, ____ it is a gas, a liquid or a solid, expands as the temperature rises.
A. whether    B. no matter    C. even if    D. whatever
答案：A

19. We must improve the farming method ____ we may get high yields.
A. therefore    B. as    C. in order that    D. of
答案：C

20. ____ the weather was fine, I opened all the windows.
A. As    B. Because of    C. For    D. With
答案：A

练习三（原书第 1–20 题，仅保留已作答题目）

1. Apart from the difficulty of getting a job, there will be the problem of finding ____ in a place where rents are so high.
A. insurance    B. installation    C. accumulation    D. accommodation
答案：D

2. The eight turbine generators will have a ____ of 6,000 million kilowatt hours.
A. quantity    B. capacity    C. capability    D. ability
答案：B

3. As is known to all, hot metal ____ as it grows cooler.
A. contracts    B. retreats    C. condenses    D. compresses
答案：A

4. The war lasted so long that people were tired of it. Every day there was large crowds of demonstrators ____ against the war.
A. protecting    B. prescribing    C. protesting    D. prosecuting
答案：C

5. Although they plant trees in this area every year, the tops of some hills are still ____.
A. blank    B. hollow    C. vacant    D. bare
答案：D

9. Why do you always ____ me to do so unpleasant jobs?
A. pick off    B. pick on    C. pick out    D. pick up
答案：B

10. They said that they had made up their minds to ____ the task in spite of the extremely difficult conditions.
A. carry out    B. carry off    C. carry on    D. carry forward
答案：A

11. ____ the students of the class passed the examination.
A. Almost all of    B. Most all of    C. Most of all    D. Almost the whole of
答案：A

12. Today's libraries differ greatly from ____.
A. the past    B. those of the past    C. those past    D. that of the past
答案：B

13. I am sorry to say that the step you have taken is ____ of great risk.
A. that    B. one    C. any    D. none
答案：B

14. Alan sold most of his belongings. He has scarcely ____ left in the house.
A. everything    B. nothing    C. something    D. anything
答案：D

15. I didn't choose any of the offerings, because I found ____ satisfactory.
A. neither of them    B. no one of them    C. either of them    D. none of them
答案：D

16. Every teacher and pupil at Eton Girls College is proud of ____ school.
A. her    B. his    C. their    D. one's
答案：A

17. Eating an apple a day is considered ____.
A. healthy    B. healthful    C. healthily    D. health
答案：B

18. The mayor of the city is a ____ old man.
A. respective    B. respectful    C. respecting    D. respectable
答案：D

19. No sooner had he gone to bed than he fell ____.
A. sleepy    B. slept    C. asleep    D. sleeping
答案：C

20. I can't come tonight. It's ____ impossible.
A. fairly    B. quite    C. very    D. rather
答案：B
